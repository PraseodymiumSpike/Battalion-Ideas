# The Reptilian Order
## Preface
I don't really like this theming much to be honest. I mostly made this because I think the Zealot would be a cool Piece, and the rest follows the format from Khan's Chess and Empire Chess.

I don't know what to do with the pawn equivalents. I don't like Berolina pawns because they, unlike normal chess pawns, don't get stuck very easily. The normal chess pawn seems to work well, so I just gave it a diagonal double-step instead of the vertical one. This might also make the Battalion focused even more on positional play, which would fit the color-locking. But I might come up with a better idea or find that this idea is really bad.

I think this Battalion might be too strong right now, but it's hard to tell.

### Zealot (Z)
The Zealot may Move or Capture an unlimited number of Spaces Forwards and to the right or Backwards and to the left along Bishop Lines; if it does so, it may also make a right-angle turn to the right at and go along another Bishop Line. It may also Move or Capture an unlimited number of Spaces Forwards and to the left or Backwards and to the right along Bishop Lines, in which case it may make a right-angle turn to the left at and go along another Bishop Line.

### Guard (G)
The Guard Moves without Capturing an unlimited number of Spaces along Bishop Lines or Captures 1 Space along Queen Lines. Outside of Party Mode, it may also swap places with a Friendly Titan as long as it is on a further-Forwards Rank than that Titan; in Party Mode, it may swap places with a Friendly Titan as long as at least 1 of the following conditions is met:
1. The Guard and the Titan it is swapping with are both in Friendly Territory, and the Guard is on a further-Forwards Rank than that Titan.
2. The Guard and the Titan it is swapping with are both in the same Enemy Territory, and the Guard is on a further-Forwards Rank than that Titan, a further-Forwards Rank being one that is closer to that Enemy's back Rank.
3. The Guard is on the Battlefield or in Enemy Territory, and the Titan it is swapping with is on the Battlefield or in Friendly Territory.

### Crocodile (C)
The Crocodile Moves without Capturing an unlimited number of Spaces along Bishop Lines or Captures an unlimited number of Spaces along Rook Lines.
### Tortoise (T)
The Tortoise Moves without Capturing 1 Space Forwards along a Rook Line and Captures 1 Space Forwards along Bishop Line; it can Capture _en passant_ like a Medieval Pawn. If on the first 2 Ranks of its Territory, it may Move without Capturing or Leaping exactly 2 Spaces Forwards along Bishop Lines; if it does so, it may be Captured _en passant_ like a Medieval Pawn&#2014;the Capturing Piece would Capture to the Space the Tortoise Moved through.
